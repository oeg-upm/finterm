### Definition
A term is "the designation of a defined concept in a special language by a linguistic expression" (ISO 1087). A term is a word or multiword expression with a specific meaning in a specialised domain.

### Domain
Finance (banking, accounting, financial markets, financial regulation).

### Rules
- Extract only terms relevant to the specified domain.
- Extract terms exactly as they appear in the text (same word form and spelling).
- If a multiword term contains shorter terms that are also terms on their own, extract both, when permitted by the annotation policy.
- Do NOT extract: numbers, dates, monetary amounts, percentages, or general-language words without a specialised meaning in the specified domain.
- If there are no terms, return an empty list.
- For this task, output specialised financial terms AND relevant named financial entities in the same terms list. Include a name only when it designates a financial institution or a directly relevant financial organisation in this sentence, such as a bank, central bank, insurer, investment fund, financial supervisor or financial-market infrastructure.
- Do not include person names, standalone geographic names or unrelated companies and organisations merely because they occur in a financial text. Keep geographic words when they are part of the complete name of a relevant financial institution.
- Preserve each selected expression exactly as it appears in the text; do not invent, translate, expand or abbreviate entity names.
